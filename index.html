    ⚠️ Important — Please Read Before Rebuilding
    When you click "Accept & Rebuild", NEXUS will create a NEW clean file — it will NOT keep your original design (fonts, colors, layout).
    • ✅ Why? ATS systems cannot read fancy designs — they reject tables, columns, and graphics.
    • ✅ Your new file will be simple, single-column, 100% ATS-readable — best chance of passing automated filters.
    • ✅ You can still edit the DOCX version in Word after download.
